# Balise: product one-pager

**What it is:** Fleet-wide correction reporting. A beacon on every Neorgon page,
one private queue, one public log of what got fixed.

**Who it is for:** Two people, and they never meet. The reader who knows one
entry is wrong and would never open a pull request to say so, because the cost of
learning how is a hundred times the cost of the correction. And the operator, who
would rather be told than find out months later that a term has been wrong since
launch.

**Register:** product (a tool someone uses), not brand (a page someone visits).

**The scene:** Someone is reading Parla on a phone, sees their own country's
slang described wrongly, and feels the small specific irritation of knowing
better. The beacon is already dimmed in the corner. One thumb, one sentence, and
they are back to reading. The other half happens later at a desk: the operator
opens the desk over coffee, reads six reports, spikes two, and writes the note
that will appear on the log.

**What done looks like:** A reader sends a correction in under thirty seconds
without making an account, and the operator turns it into a shipped fix and one
public line saying so. Neither of them ever sees the other's half.
